Built with zero compromise

Privacy is not a toggle. It's the only way financial software should exist.

Korti runs locally on your device. No open banking logins, no full card numbers, and no ad trackers.

No Bank Logins

Never hand over your online banking credentials or connect third-party open-banking aggregators.

Local Engine

Receipt rules and subscription matching run locally on-device with biometrics and PIN lock protection.

Read-Only Mail

Korti never modifies, deletes, or sends emails. Credentials remain in session memory only.

Export Anytime

Export a clean PDF summary of your cards and subscriptions or back up to your personal iCloud or Google Drive.
